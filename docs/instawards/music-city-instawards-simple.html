1. Project Summary

Music City is a music platform that helps artists document ownership splits, track qualified listening activity, and receive transparent royalty payouts through Stellar.

The working MVP already includes Stellar wallet connection, artist onboarding, track upload and streaming, subscriptions, royalty accounting, admin payout controls, and testnet USDC payments.

2. Problem and Objective

Problem

Independent artists often lack a clear, verifiable record of who owns a track and how its income should be shared. Music City currently stores royalty splits in its application database, so the active split cannot yet be independently verified on Stellar.

30-Day Objective

Deploy a Soroban royalty-split registry on Stellar Testnet, connect it to Music City, and demonstrate the complete flow from track ownership records to a verifiable testnet USDC royalty payout.

3. Deliverables

Deliverable 1 — Soroban Royalty Registry

Build and deploy a tested Soroban contract that stores versioned royalty splits for each track and confirms that all recipient shares total 100%.

Evidence: public source code, passing contract tests, testnet contract ID, and explorer transaction.

Deliverable 2 — Music City Integration

Add backend and admin tools to publish an approved split to Soroban, read it back, store its transaction details, and show whether the application record matches the on-chain record.

Evidence: repository commit, automated tests, admin screenshots, and public transaction links.

Deliverable 3 — Testnet Demo

Record a short demonstration showing the artist, track, royalty recipients, Soroban split verification, royalty ledger, and a successful testnet USDC payout.

Evidence: 3–5 minute video, screenshots, final repository commit, contract ID, payment hash, and reviewer guide.
